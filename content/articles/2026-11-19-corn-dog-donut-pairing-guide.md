The best bite at Ugly Donuts & Corn Dogs is a savory one followed by a sweet one. Pair the right corn dog with the right donut and the whole order tastes better. Here are simple pairings from our menu.

## What donut goes with a classic corn dog?

Pair the Original Corn Dog with a Nutella Strawberry Donut. The Original is rolled in panko for a light, crisp crunch, and the donut brings chocolate and fresh strawberry slices. Choose sausage, mozzarella or half and half inside the corn dog, then finish with the donut as dessert.

## What goes with a corn dog if you like heat?

Pair a Hot Cheetos Corn Dog with an Ice Cream Donut. Hot Cheetos is topped with cheddar cheese sauce and blended Hot Cheetos for a bright, crunchy kick. The Ice Cream Donut puts vanilla ice cream inside a warm donut, so it cools things down right after.

## What goes with a corn dog if you like something gently sweet?

Pair a Sweet Potato Corn Dog with a Strawberry Milk Cream Donut. The Sweet Potato Corn Dog is covered in cubed sweet potato, and it is delicious with mozzarella. The donut is filled with our homemade milk cream and strawberry slices, so both bites stay soft and creamy.

## What should you order to share?

Order two different corn dogs and a Half Dozen box of donuts. A Half Dozen lets you pick any 6 flavors, packed in our signature box. Add a Dubai Chocolate Donut, filled with pistachio and kataifi and covered in melted chocolate, and let everyone trade bites.

## Why does the pairing work so well?

Every corn dog is battered and fried to order in 100 percent avocado oil, and every donut is finished by hand after you order. Hot, crisp and savory next to warm, soft and sweet is a combination that is hard to beat. Many guests roll the corn dog in sugar and add ketchup or mustard for an extra sweet and savory mix.
