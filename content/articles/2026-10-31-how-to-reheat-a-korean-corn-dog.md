The microwave is the fastest way to turn a crispy Korean corn dog into a soft, chewy one. The good news is that a leftover corn dog can come back close to fresh if you give it dry heat and a few minutes.

A Korean corn dog is best in the first few minutes after it leaves the fryer. That is when the shell is at its crunchiest and the cheese stretches the farthest. But if you bought one for later or could not finish the second one, here is how to get it back.

## What is the best way to reheat a Korean corn dog?

Dry heat is the answer. An air fryer or a regular oven warms the center while it dries and re-crisps the shell. Steam is what makes a shell go soft, so anything that traps moisture works against you.

## How do you reheat a corn dog in an air fryer?

- Let the corn dog sit out for about 10 minutes so it is not fridge cold.
- Heat the air fryer to around 350°F.
- Lay the corn dog in a single layer with space around it.
- Heat for about 4 to 6 minutes, turning once halfway.
- Check that the center is hot all the way through before eating.

Every air fryer runs a little different, so check early the first time and add a minute if needed.

## How do you reheat a corn dog in the oven?

- Heat the oven to around 375°F.
- Set the corn dog on a wire rack over a baking tray so hot air reaches every side.
- Heat for about 8 to 12 minutes, turning once, until the center is hot.

The rack matters. A corn dog lying flat on a tray steams on the bottom and loses its crunch on that side.

## Can you reheat a corn dog in the microwave?

You can, but expect a softer shell. If the microwave is your only option, wrap the corn dog loosely in a paper towel and heat in short bursts, checking between each. For a better result, finish it for a minute or two in a hot pan or toaster oven to dry the outside.

## What about mozzarella corn dogs?

Cheese needs a little more patience. Mozzarella has to heat all the way through to stretch again, so give a mozzarella or half and half corn dog the longer end of the time ranges above. If the outside browns before the middle is warm, lower the heat a little and give it another minute or two.

## Should you add sugar or sauce before or after reheating?

After. Sugar can melt and scorch in the heat, and sauce turns a crisp shell soft. Reheat the corn dog plain, then roll it in sugar or add your ketchup and mustard right before you eat. If yours already had sugar on it, that is fine, just keep an eye on it so it does not darken too much.

## How long do leftover corn dogs keep?

Refrigerate leftovers in a sealed container and eat them within a day or two for the best texture. Do not leave a corn dog sitting out at room temperature for hours before saving it.

## Why fresh is still the best version

Reheating can get you close, but nothing beats a corn dog straight from the fryer. That is why every Korean corn dog we serve is battered when you order and fried in 100 percent avocado oil. When you can, eat the first one at the counter and save the reheating tricks for the one you take home.
