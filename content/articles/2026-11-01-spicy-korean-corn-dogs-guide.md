Bright red crunch, Korean fire sauce, or sweet heat with peanuts. Our spicy corn dogs each bring a different kind of kick, and picking one is half the fun. Here is what goes on each one, so you know exactly what you are biting into.

## What makes a Korean corn dog spicy at Ugly Donuts?

Every corn dog starts the same way. We batter it when you order and fry it in 100 percent avocado oil, so the shell comes out crispy and golden. The heat comes from what we add on top. Each spicy corn dog has its own topping combination, and each one hits differently.

## What is on the Hot Cheetos Corn Dog?

The Hot Cheetos Corn Dog is topped with cheddar cheese sauce and blended Hot Cheetos. The cheese sauce gives you a creamy layer, and the blended Hot Cheetos add a bright red crunch with that familiar spicy, cheesy snap. It is one of our best sellers, and one look explains why.

Pick it if you love crunch first and heat second.

## What is on the Buldak Corn Dog?

The Buldak Corn Dog is topped with spicy Korean buldak sauce and Korean seaweed bites. Buldak is the Korean fire sauce many fans know from spicy noodles, and the seaweed bites add a savory, toasty crunch on top.

Pick it if you want a true Korean flavor with bold, saucy heat.

## What is on the Dynamite Corn Dog?

The Dynamite Corn Dog is topped with our Korean sweet and spicy in-house made sauce, chopped peanuts and parsley. The sauce brings sweetness and heat together, the peanuts add a nutty crunch, and the parsley finishes it fresh.

Pick it if you like sweet heat with a little crunch in every bite.

## What about the Blue Takis Corn Dog?

Our Fall Seasonal Blue Takis Corn Dog is topped with creamy cheese sauce and crushed Blue Takis. The blue color stops people in line, and the crushed Takis bring a bold, tangy crunch. It is a seasonal item, so try it while it is on the menu.

Pick it if you want the most eye catching corn dog in the case.

## Which filling should you choose?

Every spicy corn dog comes with your choice of filling:

- **Sausage** for a classic, savory bite
- **Mozzarella** for the long cheese pull
- **Half and half** for sausage and mozzarella in one stick

Mozzarella pairs well with the Hot Cheetos and Blue Takis corn dogs if you want extra cheese. Sausage stands up nicely to the bold buldak sauce. Half and half is the easy answer when you cannot decide.

## How do you pick if you are new to spicy corn dogs?

Start with the topping style you already love. If you reach for spicy chips, go Hot Cheetos or Blue Takis. If you love Korean spicy noodles, go Buldak. If you like sweet and spicy wings, go Dynamite. Coming with friends? Order a few and share, so everyone gets a taste of each.

## Quick FAQ

**Can I choose the filling on a spicy corn dog?**
Yes. Choose sausage, mozzarella or half and half.
