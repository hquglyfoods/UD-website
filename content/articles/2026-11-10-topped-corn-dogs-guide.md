Crunch first, then the topping hits. That is the moment our topped corn dogs are built for. Each one starts as a Korean corn dog battered and fried to order in 100 percent avocado oil, then gets finished with its own toppings. Here is what is on each one, so you know exactly what you are getting.

## What is a topped corn dog at Ugly Donuts?

A topped corn dog is one of our Korean corn dogs finished with sauces and toppings after it comes out of the fryer. You still pick your favorite inner filling, and the toppings turn it into a full flavor of its own.

## What is on the New Yorker Corn Dog?

**New Yorker Corn Dog**
Topped with relish, fried onion flakes and onion powder. Think of a classic New York street hot dog, rebuilt as a crispy Korean corn dog. The relish brings a bright, tangy bite and the fried onion flakes add an extra layer of crunch.

## What is on The Guac Corn Dog?

**The Guac Corn Dog**
Topped with guacamole, bacon crumbs and shredded cheese. Creamy, smoky and savory all at once. If you like loaded nachos, this is the one to try.

## What is on the Premium Pizza Corn Dog?

**Premium Pizza Corn Dog**
Topped with pizza sauce, basil pesto and garlic powder. It tastes like a slice and a corn dog in the same bite. Pick a mozzarella filling for a cheese pull that finishes the pizza idea.

## What is the Blue Takis Corn Dog?

**Blue Takis Corn Dog (Fall Seasonal)**
Coated in creamy cheese sauce and crushed Blue Takis for a bold, crunchy kick. It is on the menu for fall, so try it while it is here.

## Which topped corn dogs are spicy?

If you want heat, look at our Hot Cheetos, Buldak and Dynamite Corn Dogs. We wrote a full guide to our spicy Korean corn dogs on the Journal. The New Yorker, The Guac and the Premium Pizza Corn Dog are savory picks for anyone who wants big flavor without the burn.

## How do you pick a filling for a topped corn dog?

Every topped corn dog comes with your choice of inner filling. Sausage gives a savory snap that stands up to bold toppings like relish and onion. Mozzarella gives the famous cheese pull, and it pairs naturally with the pizza and guac toppings. Half and half gives you both.
