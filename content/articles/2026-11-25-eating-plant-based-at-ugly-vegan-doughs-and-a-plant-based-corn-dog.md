A corn dog shop might sound like the last place for a plant based eater. At Ugly Donuts and Corn Dogs, it is not. Both our corn dog dough and our donut dough are vegan, and most of our corn dogs can be made with a plant based sausage option. That means a fully plant based corn dog is on the menu, made to order just like everything else we serve.



\## What makes a corn dog plant based?



It starts with the dough. Our corn dog dough is vegan, so the crisp shell around every corn dog is already plant based. The filling is the part you choose. Pick the plant based sausage option instead of the regular sausage or mozzarella, and the corn dog is plant based from the inside out. Everything is fried in 100 percent avocado oil and finished to order, so your corn dog comes off the fryer hot and crisp.



\## Which corn dog should you order?



Start with the Original Corn Dog. It is rolled in panko for a light, crisp crunch, and it shows off the dough and the filling with nothing in the way. From there, pick the coating you like best. The Potato Corn Dog is covered in crispy potato cubes for a heartier bite. The Sweet Potato Corn Dog brings a soft sweetness to the shell. The Nude Corn Dog skips the crunchy coating for a soft, chewy shell.



Choose the plant based sausage filling inside whichever coating you pick. If you are ordering for a mixed group, half and half is a good call for everyone else, since one stick gives a taste of both the savory snap and the cheese pull, while your plant based corn dog stays its own order.



\## How do you finish it?



A Korean corn dog is finished at the counter, so say what you want when you order. The classic finish is a roll in sugar, and ketchup and mustard are on hand too. Ask at the kiosk which sauces and toppings work with a plant based order, and the team will help you finish it the way you like.



\## What about the donuts?



Our donut dough is vegan too. Donuts are cut in half before filling, so every donut carries its filling all the way through. Fillings and toppings vary, so ask at the kiosk which donuts work for a plant based order on the day you visit. Pair your corn dog and donut with a drink from our menu, like a boba or a lemonade, and you have a full spread without cooking a thing.



\## How do you order?



Order at the kiosk in store and pick your mix on the spot. Tell the team you would like the plant based sausage option, pick your coating, and choose your finish. Items vary by location, so check the menu for your nearest store on our locations page before you visit.



\## Quick FAQ



\*\*Is the corn dog dough vegan?\*\* Yes. Both our corn dog dough and our donut dough are vegan.



\*\*Can I get a fully plant based corn dog?\*\* Yes. Pick any coating and choose the plant based sausage filling, and ask at the kiosk about sauces and toppings.



\*\*Which coatings can I choose?\*\* Original, Potato, Sweet Potato or Nude. All four start from the same vegan dough.



\*\*What should I ask at the kiosk?\*\* Ask for the plant based sausage option and which sauces and toppings work with a plant based order that day.



\*\*What do you fry in?\*\* Everything is fried in 100 percent avocado oil.
