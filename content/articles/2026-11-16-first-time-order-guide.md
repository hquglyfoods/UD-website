Start with one corn dog and one donut. That sweet and savory pair is the fastest way to understand what we do, and it works whether you are ordering for yourself or for the whole table. Here are easy first-visit orders for one, two or four people.

## What should you order on your first visit?

Pick one corn dog and one donut. Every corn dog is battered and fried to order in 100 percent avocado oil, and every donut is finished by hand after you order, so both arrive fresh and hot. Our menu has about 98 percent made to order items, which means what you get is made for you in that moment.

## What is the best first order for one person?

Go with a classic corn dog and a filled donut.

- **Corn dog:** The Original Corn Dog is rolled in panko for a light, crisp crunch. If you want more bite, the Potato Corn Dog is one of our best sellers.
- **Filling:** Choose sausage for a savory snap, mozzarella for the long cheese pull, or half and half for both.
- **Donut:** The Nutella Strawberry Donut is one of our best sellers, with chocolate and fresh strawberry slices in the same bite.

Eat the corn dog first while the coating is at its crispiest, then finish with the donut as dessert. Many guests roll the corn dog in sugar and add ketchup or mustard.

## What is the best order for two people?

Share two different corn dogs and two different donuts, so each of you gets a taste of everything.

- **Savory and bold:** The Hot Cheetos Corn Dog, topped with cheddar cheese sauce and blended Hot Cheetos, is one of our guest favorites.
- **Savory and classic:** The Sweet Potato Corn Dog is covered in cubed sweet potato for a gentle sweetness. Pair it with mozzarella.
- **Sweet and creamy:** The Strawberry Milk Cream Donut is filled with our homemade milk cream and strawberry slices.
- **Sweet and cold:** An Ice Cream Donut puts vanilla ice cream inside a warm donut. The Donut Split comes with strawberry, banana and a Nutella drizzle.

Cut each one in half and swap. It is the easiest way to find your favorite.

## What is the best order for a group of four?

Order a mix of corn dogs and a Half Dozen box of donuts.

- **Corn dogs:** Choose four different ones, such as the Original, the Potato, the New Yorker and the Buldak. Each comes with your choice of sausage, mozzarella or half and half.
- **Donuts:** A Half Dozen lets you pick any 6 flavors, packed in our signature box. Add a Dubai Chocolate Donut, filled with pistachio and kataifi and covered in melted chocolate, and an Oreo Cheesecake Donut for the cookie fans.

Set everything in the middle of the table and let everyone try a bite of each.

## Which corn dog should you pick if you like heat?

Look at our Hot Cheetos, Buldak and Dynamite Corn Dogs. Hot Cheetos brings a bright red crunch with cheese sauce. Buldak brings Korean fire sauce and seaweed bites. Dynamite is a sweet and spicy in-house sauce with chopped peanuts. We wrote a full guide to our spicy Korean corn dogs on the Journal.
