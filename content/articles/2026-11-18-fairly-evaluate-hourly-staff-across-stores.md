We use Wevree at our Forest Hills, Bushwick, and American Dream stores, with about 30 staff across those three locations. Wevree is staff management for teams, from cafes to offices and retail. We use it for peer evaluation, so hourly staff are reviewed by the coworkers who actually work the same shift, not only by a manager who happened to visit.

Every store makes Korean corn dogs and hot donuts to order, fries them in 100 percent avocado oil, and mixes fresh dough by hand every morning. The hard part is not the recipe. It is making sure hourly staff at Forest Hills, Bushwick, and American Dream are evaluated fairly, even when we are not standing in that store.

## How do we fairly evaluate hourly staff across our stores?

A manager visiting a store sees a snapshot. The people who see the real pattern are the coworkers on that same shift. That is why we use peer evaluation for hourly teams. [The way Wevree runs peer evaluation](https://www.wevree.com/blog/how-to-fairly-evaluate-hourly-restaurant-staff/) turns that daily knowledge into a review a store manager can actually use.

All three stores use the same process and the same 1 to 10 scale, so a review in Forest Hills is built the same way as a review in Bushwick or at American Dream. We picked Wevree in part because [running staff reviews the same way across multiple locations](https://www.wevree.com/blog/multi-location-staff-performance-software/) is something we did not want to rebuild separately in each store.

## What changed after we started using Wevree?

Here is what we can say from our own records and from the owner's own account.

We have about 30 staff across these three stores. Before Wevree, 2 to 3 staff changed or quit each month. In the 8 weeks after starting, only 1 employee left.

All 3 stores had a week with zero late employees, the first in 5 years. Punctuality is one of the rated items.

At American Dream, after the first rating cycle, an employee asked coworkers on his own how to improve. His customer service score went from 6.67 to 8.14 over 5 rounds, without owner intervention.

Staff kept working hard consistently and teamwork improved.

By the owner's own account, overseeing one store used to take about 30 hours a week. Since using Wevree, that is about 2 to 3 hours a week.

In an anonymous staff survey in October 2026, 27 of our 30 staff responded, and more than 90% said Wevree helps a lot with teamwork and their own growth.

Once we started, it felt like we could never stop using it. That is the owner's opinion, not a promise about every team.
