Warm donut on the outside, cold vanilla ice cream in the middle. That first bite is the whole reason our Ice Cream Donuts exist. Here are all five, so you know exactly which one to order next time.

## What is an Ice Cream Donut?

An Ice Cream Donut is one of our hot donuts filled with vanilla ice cream, served in a cup while the donut is still warm. Every donut is finished by hand after you order, and our donuts are fried in 100 percent avocado oil, just like our corn dogs.

## Which Ice Cream Donuts are on the menu?

**Original Ice Cream Donut**
The classic. A hot donut with vanilla ice cream. Simple, warm and cold in every bite, and the best place to start.

**Donut Split**
Our take on a banana split. The Ice Cream Donut is topped with fresh strawberry and banana slices, then drizzled with Nutella.

**Loaded Lotus Ice Cream Donut**
For cookie butter fans. Topped with Lotus Biscoff bites and a caramel drizzle, so you get crunch, caramel and cream together.

**Nutella Overload Ice Cream Donut**
For serious chocolate lovers. Nutella drizzle, chocolate crunch balls and Ovomaltine, a crunchy chocolate powder, all on top of the ice cream.

**Oreos & Cream Ice Cream Donut**
Cookies and cream, donut style. Topped with Oreo bites and condensed milk.

## Which one should you pick?

Go with the topping you already love.

- **Fruit fan:** Donut Split
- **Caramel and cookie butter:** Loaded Lotus
- **All the chocolate:** Nutella Overload
- **Cookies and cream:** Oreos & Cream
- **Keep it classic:** Original

Coming with friends? Order a few different ones and share. Pair an Ice Cream Donut with a corn dog for the full sweet and savory Ugly experience.

## Can you eat ice cream donuts in the fall and winter?

Yes, and they might be even better. The donut comes to you hot, so the warm dough and cold ice cream balance each other out. It is a dessert that works in any season.
