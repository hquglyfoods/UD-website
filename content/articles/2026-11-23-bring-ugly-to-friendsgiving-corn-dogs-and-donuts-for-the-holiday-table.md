Friendsgiving is the holiday meal with no seating chart and no dress code. Everyone brings something, everyone stands around the table, and the best dishes are the ones people can grab, share and trade. That is exactly where our corn dogs and donuts fit. Here is how to build a Friendsgiving spread from our menu.

## What should you bring to Friendsgiving?

Bring one savory crowd pleaser and one sweet one. A few corn dogs cover the savory side, and a Half Dozen box of donuts covers dessert. Both travel well, both are easy to share, and both give the table something to talk about.

## Which corn dogs work best for a group?

Pick three that taste different from each other, so everyone finds a favorite. The Original Corn Dog is the safe start, rolled in panko for a light, crisp crunch. Add the Potato Corn Dog, covered in crispy potato cubes, for a heartier bite. Then add one with personality, like the Hot Cheetos Corn Dog, topped with cheddar cheese sauce and blended Hot Cheetos, or the Guac Corn Dog, with guacamole and bacon.

Choose sausage, mozzarella or half and half inside each corn dog. Half and half is a good call for a group, since one stick gives everyone a taste of both the savory snap and the cheese pull.

## What about guests who want a plant based option?

Our corn dog and donut doughs are vegan, and most corn dogs can be made with a plant based sausage option. Ask at the kiosk when you order and the team will help you put together a mix that works for the whole table.

## What donuts should go on the dessert table?

A Half Dozen box lets you pick any 6 flavors, packed in our signature box. For a holiday table, mix filled donuts with something rich. The Nutella Strawberry Donut, filled with Nutella and fresh strawberry slices, and the Oreo Cheesecake Donut are easy favorites. Add the Dubai Chocolate Donut, filled with pistachio and kataifi and covered in melted chocolate, as the one everyone reaches for first.

Our donuts are cut in half before filling, so every donut carries its filling all the way through. Set the box in the middle of the table and let people trade halves.

## What should you serve with them?

Corn dogs and donuts are both finger food, so keep the rest of the table simple. Add a drink from our menu, like a boba or a lemonade, and you have a full spread without cooking a thing. Everything is fried and finished to order in 100 percent avocado oil, so order close to your gathering time and serve while the corn dogs are still crisp.

## How do you order for a group?

Order at the kiosk in store and pick your mix on the spot. If your group is large, order a few extra corn dogs and a second Half Dozen box. Items vary by location, so check the menu for your nearest store on our locations page before you visit.

## Quick FAQ

**Are the corn dogs and donuts made to order?** Yes. Every corn dog is battered and fried to order, and every donut is finished by hand after you order.

**Can I get a mix of corn dogs for a group?** Yes. Pick different coatings and fillings, and choose sausage, mozzarella or half and half inside each one.

**Is there a plant based option?** Yes. Both doughs are vegan, and most corn dogs can be made with a plant based sausage option. Ask at the kiosk when you order.

**What do you fry in?** Everything is fried in 100 percent avocado oil.
