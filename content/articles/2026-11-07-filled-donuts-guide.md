Bite in, and the filling meets you halfway. That is the whole idea behind our filled donuts. Each one is finished by hand after you order, and each one has its own personality. Here is what is inside every one, so you know exactly what you are getting.

## What is a filled donut at Ugly Donuts?

A filled donut is one of our soft donuts packed with a filling, from Nutella to homemade milk cream. Our donuts are fried in 100 percent avocado oil, just like our corn dogs, and finished when you order, so the filling is generous and the donut is fresh.

## Which Nutella donuts are on the menu?

**Nutella Donut**
A donut filled with Nutella. Rich, chocolatey and simple. If you love Nutella, start here.

**Nutella Strawberry Donut**
A donut filled with Nutella and strawberry slices. Chocolate and fresh fruit in the same bite, and one of our best sellers.

**Nutella Banana Donut**
A donut filled with Nutella and sliced bananas. Think of your favorite banana and chocolate snack, inside a warm donut.

## What is in the Milk Cream Donut?

The Milk Cream Donut is filled with our homemade milk cream. It is light, milky and smooth, a favorite for anyone who wants something sweet that still feels soft and gentle.

**Strawberry Milk Cream Donut**
The same homemade milk cream, plus strawberry slices. Creamy and fruity, and very pretty when you cut it in half.

## Which filled donut is best for cheesecake fans?

The Oreo Cheesecake Donut. It is filled with Oreo pieces and our special Oreo cheese filling. Creamy, crunchy and cookie packed in every bite.

## What about Dulce de Leche and Dubai Chocolate?

**Dulce de Leche Donut**
A donut filled with dulce de leche and lotus crumbs. Caramel sweetness with a cookie crunch on top.

**Dubai Chocolate Donut**
A donut filled with pistachio and kataifi, covered with melted chocolate. The viral flavor, in donut form, with that crispy pistachio crunch inside.

## How do I pick the right filled donut?

- **Chocolate lover:** Nutella Donut or Dubai Chocolate Donut
- **Fruit and chocolate:** Nutella Strawberry or Nutella Banana
- **Light and creamy:** Milk Cream or Strawberry Milk Cream
- **Cookie crunch:** Oreo Cheesecake or Dulce de Leche

Can't decide? Grab a Half Dozen. You can pick any 6 of your favorite flavors, packed in our signature half dozen box. It is the easiest way to try them all with friends.
