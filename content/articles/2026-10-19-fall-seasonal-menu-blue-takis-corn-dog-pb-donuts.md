Bright blue crunch on a golden corn dog. That is the first thing people notice about our fall lineup, and it is exactly the point.

Fall is when cravings get louder. Cooler air, longer evenings, and a stronger pull toward something rich, crunchy, and a little nostalgic. Our fall seasonal menu leans into all three with one bold corn dog and two peanut butter donuts.

## The Blue Takis Corn Dog

This one is for the spice and crunch crowd. We start with a crispy Korean corn dog, coat it in creamy cheese sauce, and finish it with crushed Blue Takis for a bold, crunchy kick.

You still choose your favorite filling, so it can be as classic or as stretchy as you want. Every corn dog is battered when you order it and fried in 100 percent avocado oil, so the shell is hot and crisp when it reaches you, not sitting in a warming case.

Fair warning: the color is part of the fun. Grab a napkin.

## The PB and Jelly Donut

Remember the sandwich you traded for at lunch? This is the grown up version. Peanut cream pairs with sliced strawberries and a luscious strawberry syrup to bring a classic PB and J flavor to life.

It is rich, sweet, and fruity in one bite, built on our handmade donut with dough mixed fresh every morning.

## The Banana PB Toffee Donut

If you are a peanut butter loyalist, start here. Rich peanut cream is layered with fresh banana slices and crunchy toffee bites for a sweet, creamy combination with a little snap on top.

Both fall donuts contain peanuts, so please let our team know about any allergies before you order.

## How to build the perfect fall order

Want the full seasonal experience? Try this:

- One Blue Takis Corn Dog for the savory, crunchy hit
- One PB and Jelly Donut or Banana PB Toffee Donut for dessert
- One of our signature refreshers or a bubble tea, made in house, to balance it all out

Sharing with friends? A half dozen box lets everyone pick their own donut flavors, seasonal ones included.
